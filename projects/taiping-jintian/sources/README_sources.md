# Sources — provenance and reliability

Question: 太平天国运动最开始爆发的地方（和时间）在哪里？
*Where (and when) did the Taiping movement first break out as an armed uprising?*

## Source 1 (secondary — no OCR needed)

**中国国家博物馆 (National Museum of China), "金田起义"** event page.

Quote used:
> "1851年1月11日（道光三十年十二月十日），洪秀全和杨秀清、肖朝贵、冯云山、韦昌辉、石达开等人率众约两万人在**广西桂平金田村**发动武装起义，建号太平天国。"

URL: https://www.chnmuseum.cn/zp/zpml/ysp/202012/t20201214_248369.shtml

Why reliable: a national museum's institutional description of the event it commemorates (the
Jintian site is a protected national heritage site). It states the precise place — **广西桂平金田村**.
It is a **secondary** source, so it is paired with the contemporary source below.

**Corroboration (same claim, different source):** Encyclopaedia Britannica, "Taiping Rebellion" —
the God Worshippers' Society "he formed among the impoverished peasants of Guangxi province … he
led them in rebellion"; the rebellion "began in the provinces of Guangdong and Guangxi."
https://www.britannica.com/event/Taiping-Rebellion

## Source 2 (needs OCR — the OCR'd source)

**Thomas Taylor Meadows, *The Chinese and Their Rebellions* (London: Smith, Elder, 1856), printed
p. 5.** A contemporary British account written by a consular interpreter in China.

Digitised (public domain) on Internet Archive: identifier `chineseandtheir01meadgoog`;
page scan used: `sources/raw/meadows_n77.jpg` (leaf n77 = printed p.5), **unchanged**.

OCR'd text: `sources/processed/page-05_vision-ocr.md`; comparison with IA machine OCR in
`ocr/ocr_results_comparison.md`.

Quote used (origin of the movement):
> "The province of Kwangse, in which the present religious movement took its rise, contains the
> most of these [aboriginal mountaineers]."

Why reliable: a contemporary (1856) eyewitness-informed account by a British official serving in
China, close to the events, and one of the foundational Western sources on the movement. Caveat:
British-imperial vantage point; it gives the **province** (Kwangse = Guangxi), not the village, so
the precise location rests on Source 1.

## Why the two sources together answer the question

- Source 2 (primary, OCR'd) establishes that the movement arose in **Kwangse (Guangxi)** — from a
  contemporary who was on the ground.
- Source 1 (secondary, institutional) fixes the precise place and date: **广西桂平金田村，
  1851年1月11日** (Jintian village, Guiping county, Guangxi, 11 January 1851 — the Jintian Uprising).
- Together: the uprising first broke out in **Jintian village, Guiping, Guangxi, on 11 Jan 1851**;
  its organisational base (God Worshipping Society, Thistle Mount / 紫荆山) and the "团营" assembly
  (July 1850) were also in Guiping, Guangxi.

## Note on the term "爆发" (outbreak)

Distinguish three moments, which is itself part of the historical judgment:
1. 拜上帝会 founded in **广东花县** (Hong Xiuquan's home), 1843 — organisation, not outbreak.
2. 团营 order, July 1850 — members converge on **金田村**, Guangxi.
3. **金田起义**, 11 Jan 1851 — the armed uprising / 建号太平天国. This is the "爆发" point.
